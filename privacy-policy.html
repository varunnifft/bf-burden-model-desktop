BF Model privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

BF Model (Android app com.bfburdenmodel.app and the Windows and Mac apps) is published by Niteen Kumar. Contact: varunnifft@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: name, mobile number, email address, company, the login ID you choose, and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself). Used to create and secure your login, to send OTP emails and quiz results, and to contact you about your suggestions.
  • App use: date of registration, last login, number of logins, last time the app checked your login, and the app platform and version.
  • Quiz results: when you take a quiz posted in the app, the quiz form (Google Forms) records your answers and email; the app owner's records keep your score, the quiz name and the time, and email you your score.
  • Suggestions: the text you send, its type and the page it is about, with your name, email, company and login ID so the owner can reply.
  • Payments: the Report and Other charging modes pages are paid. On Android you pay through Google Play; on Windows and Mac through Razorpay. The payment provider handles your card, UPI or bank details; the app owner's records keep only your login ID, name, email, what you bought, the amount, the payment ID and until when it is valid.

What stays on your device

Furnace inputs, lab analyses you import, calculator and simulator values and reports are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded. Files you load from a link are downloaded to your device and read there.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Account, quiz and suggestion data are kept in a Google Sheet in the app owner's Google account (Google Apps Script and Google Forms, run by Google). Only the app owner can see them. We do not sell your data. The app has no analytics. Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS.

Ads

The Android app shows Google ads (Google AdMob) on the Fact must know page. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions, under Google's policy. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google > Ads). The Windows and Mac apps show no Google ads.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and quiz results at any time in the app (Home page, Delete account) or on this page. Suggestions you sent are then kept without your name or contact details. Logging out removes your login from the device; uninstalling the app removes everything saved on the device.

Children

BF Model is an engineering tool for adults working in ironmaking. It is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes.
