How it is calculated

Methods, equations and limits, for learners and reviewers

1 · Tuyere and raceway

Raceway flame temperature (RAFT) from a heat balance per minute: hot blast sensible heat + coke carbon entering at 1500 °C + C → CO (110.5 kJ/mol) − H₂O + C → CO + H₂ (131.3 kJ/mol) − PCI heating and decomposition ≈ (250 + 32·VM) kJ/kg = Σ n·ΔH(T) of CO, H₂, N₂ and ash at the flame temperature. Tuyere velocity v = Q·(T/273)·(1.033/(1.033 + BP)) / A; kinetic energy ½·ṁ·v² per tuyere; raceway depth from the kinetic energy (empirical, indicative).

2 · Carbon, oxygen and hydrogen balance

Carbon to gas = coke + nut coke + PCI carbon − carbon in hot metal. Oxygen to gas = blast O + coal O + oxygen removed from the iron oxides (from burden Fe and FeO) + Si and Mn reduction − O taken by H₂. CO₂ = O − C, CO = C − CO₂, so ηCO = CO₂/(CO + CO₂), with ηH₂ ≈ 0.88·ηCO. Direct reduction = (carbon to gas − carbon burnt at the tuyeres − Si/Mn) ÷ oxygen in FeO.

3 · Gas distribution

Every ring of the calculator's burden distribution is a parallel path. Per metre: ΔP/L = A·μ·u + B·ρ·u² (Ergun), with A = 150(1−ε)²/(ε³d²) and B = 1.75(1−ε)/(ε³d), for ore and coke layers in series. At each level the gas splits so that all rings have the same pressure gradient; in the cohesive zone only the coke slits are open; near the tuyeres the raceway and deadman set the split.

4 · Temperatures

In every ring the gas (up) and burden (down) exchange heat: ṁgcgdTg = −hv(Tg−Ts)dV and ṁsdHs = hv(Tg−Ts)dV, with the burden enthalpy Hs(T) holding the heat sinks (moisture, flux calcination, direct reduction and solution loss, melting, Si reduction) and the cooling loss at the wall. Solved implicitly level by level and iterated with the gas split until it settles. The cohesive zone lies between the burden's softening start and melting end temperatures.

5 · PCI in the raceway

Five particle sizes from the grind (Rosin–Rammler through the mean size and the −75 µm share), followed from the lance tip: drag to the gas speed, heating by convection (Nu = 2 + 0.6 Re½Pr⅓) and radiation; volatiles dV/dt = Ave−E/RT(V* − V) with V* = Q × proximate VM; char burning by O₂ with kinetics and film diffusion in series, q = pO₂/(1/ks + 1/kd), kd = C₁·Tm0.75/d (Baum and Street form), then CO₂ gasification. The gas temperature comes from an enthalpy balance at every step with the volatiles, char and the coke at the raceway boundary.

Limits

Reduced-order engineering models, not CFD; axisymmetric in the furnace, one representative tuyere in the raceway. Kinetic and heat-transfer constants are starting values from the literature: fit them to your probe, top gas, pressure and burnout readings ("Fit to my measurements", calibration rows) and compare cases rather than trusting single absolute values. Tapping is assumed good and all coal lances equal.

Simulation outputs

Indicative model. Fit it to your probe, top gas and shaft readings before you act on absolute numbers; trends between two cases are more reliable than single values.
