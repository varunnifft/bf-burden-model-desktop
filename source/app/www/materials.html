How they interact in the furnace

Burden fines (sinter, ore) raise the pressure drop in the stack, while weak coke (low CSR) loses its open "windows" in the cohesive zone and the deadman fills with fines. Together they cause gas channelling, unstable descent and a higher coke rate. Good practice is a screened burden (little under 5 mm), CSR 62–70 % or more, low alkali input, and a balanced mix so that no single material dominates the degradation.
