What each page takes

Furnace 3D view reads geometry, blast, burden and fuel rates from the Burden calculator and Batch chemistry, and PCI burnout from its own raceway model. While the calculator is linked to Batch chemistry, burden per charge and moisture come from the charge and lab analysis. So those inputs appear only once.
