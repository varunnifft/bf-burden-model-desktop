Delete your BF Model account

BF Model (Android app com.bfburdenmodel.app, Windows and Mac apps) by Niteen Kumar.

What is deleted

Your login and profile (name, mobile number, email address, company, login ID and password hash) and any quiz results are deleted from the app owner's records straight away. Suggestions you sent are kept without your name or contact details. Payment records that the law requires us to keep (amount, date, payment ID) are kept for that period only. Furnace data is never uploaded, so there is nothing of it to delete on our side; uninstalling the app removes it from your device.

Three ways to delete

  1. In the app: Home page, Delete account at the bottom, then your password.
  2. On this page: enter your login ID (or email) and password below.
  3. By email: write to varunnifft@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my BF Model account". It is done within 7 days.

Delete now

Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password" in the app first, or use the email option above.
